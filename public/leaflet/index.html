About Our School

Mbarara Army Boarding Secondary School is a premier institution that combines military discipline with academic excellence to provide holistic education. We nurture students to become responsible citizens, critical thinkers, and future leaders. Our unique approach fosters self-discipline, integrity, and patriotism while maintaining the highest standards of academic achievement.

With state-of-the-art facilities and a dedicated teaching staff, we offer a conducive learning environment that prepares students for both national examinations and real-world challenges.

Levels / Classes Offered

O-Level (S1 – S4)

Four-year comprehensive secondary education program following the Ugandan National Curriculum.

A-Level (S5 – S6)

Advanced secondary education with specialized combinations in Sciences, Arts, and Humanities.

Admission Requirements

For S1 Entry

  • Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) Results
  • Learner Identification Number (LIN)
  • Birth Certificate
  • Recommendation letter from previous school
  • 4 recent passport-size photographs
  • Medical examination report

For S5 Entry

  • Learner Identification Number (LIN)
  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) Slip
  • Birth Certificate
  • Recommendation letter from previous school
  • 4 recent passport-size photographs
  • Medical examination report

General Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Interview with admission panel
  • Admission test (for some classes)
  • Copy of parents'/guardians' IDs
