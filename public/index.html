About Mbarara Army Boarding Secondary School

Mbarara Army Boarding Secondary School (MABSS) was established in 2004 with the aim of providing quality education to children of fallen & serving soldiers with military discipline and patriotic values. All children are admitted regardless of background.

Located on the scenic hills of Makenke, Mbarara, along the Mbarara-Masaka Highway, the school began with 120 students and 15 staff members. Today, it accommodates over 2,000 students drawn from various regions of Uganda.

The school's unique approach combines rigorous academic training with military-style discipline, leadership development, and patriotism. This model consistently produces graduates who excel academically while demonstrating integrity and service to the nation.

MABSS stands as one of the premier educational institutions in the region, recognized for strong performance in national examinations and shaping disciplined citizens.
